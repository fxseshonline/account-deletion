FX Sesh Account Deletion Request

Introduction

We take your privacy seriously. If you would like to delete your FX Sesh account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Send an email to fxseshonline@gmail.com with the subject line: "Delete My FX Sesh Account".
  2. Include your account email or username in the message.
  3. Our team will process your request within 30 days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (email, password, Google sign-in info)
  • Journal entries and attached screenshots/images
  • Notification preferences and settings

Data That Will Be Retained

  • Anonymous analytics data in aggregated form for app improvement (up to 26 months)
  • Crash reports and usage analytics (up to 90 days)

Partial Deletion Requests (Optional)

You may request deletion of specific types of data (e.g., only journal entries) by specifying your request in the email.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding your data or deletion requests, please contact us at fxseshonline@gmail.com.

⚠️ Important: Please note that once your account is deleted, all associated journal entries and settings will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
